Note. Contrasts are on the log OR scale at a common marginal-median background. RMS bias is sqrt(mean over evaluation points of squared repetition-mean error). RMSE is sqrt(mean squared error over all usable repetitions and points). MCSE uses deletion of an entire repetition; the empirical SD is summarized on its root-mean-square scale. Identically zero anchor contrasts are excluded; other zero true effects enter the absolute-error summaries. Usable/planned counts are reported separately for each component. Independent repetitions determine Monte Carlo uncertainty. Fit scenario records the CLR adjustment fit and its original independent-repetition count. A single-exposure curve describes a fixed-background contrast, including any interactions at that background, rather than a unique main-effect decomposition.
