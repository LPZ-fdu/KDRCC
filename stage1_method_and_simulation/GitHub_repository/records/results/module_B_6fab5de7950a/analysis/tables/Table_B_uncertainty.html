Note. Empirical SD is computed across independent repetitions at each fixed point and summarized by its root mean square. Model SE is summarized on the same squared scale. SE ratio is model SE divided by empirical SD; its reference value is one. Coverage is the average pointwise coverage of nominal 95% intervals and is a secondary assessment. Intervals condition on the independently learned kernel; approximation and regularization bias are assessed separately through their empirical errors and coverage. Empirical variability across repetitions can also include kernel learning. Width and coverage must be interpreted together with bias and estimation error.
