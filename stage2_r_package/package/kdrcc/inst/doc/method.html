KDR-CC statistical specification

For a matched set with one event and m reference dates, each event/reference comparison is represented in both orders with weight 1/(2m). D identifies an event in the first position. Z is the difference of centered, whitened Gaussian features; S is the difference of adjustment features. Complete sets define independent analysis units.

The response is h(x)=phi(x)' beta with phi(x)=[K(x,c)-K(0,c)] B and K(x,c)=exp(-sum_j rho_j (z_j-c_j)^2). Exposures are standardized using independently learned centers and scales. Outcome-based profile likelihood learns positive distance parameters rho. Weighted k-means++ and farthest-point augmentation supply kernel centers. Within-set whitening fixes the coefficient coordinates.

For an ordered comparison, eta=Z' beta+g(W). The base moment is psi={D exp(-eta)-(1-D)}{Z-f(W)}. The expectation is under the normalized weighted ordered-pair law. Here f(W)=E[Z|D=0,W]. If the logistic pair model is correct and beta equals the represented true response, conditional odds equal exp(Z' beta+g0(W)). With g=g0 the conditional mean of the first factor given Z,W is zero. With f=f0, its mean given W is proportional to E[(Z-f0(W))|D=0,W]=0, for every chosen g(W). This gives the base moment's double-robust identity.

The independent learning sample fixes kernel geometry, auxiliary dictionaries, the moment transformation T and mixed-derivative matrix P. K-fold matched-set cross-fitting gives U_N(beta)=N^-1 sum_i psi_i(beta,eta_hat_-fold(i)). The final criterion is Q_N(beta)=||T U_N(beta)||^2/2+lambda beta' P beta/2. Its gradient is J' T'T U_N+lambda P beta. The solver uses damped Gauss-Newton directions and an Armijo line search; stopping requires both maximum-gradient and relative-step tolerances. Exact local curvature adds sum_a (T'T U_N)_a Hessian(U_N,a) to J'T'TJ+lambda P.

For fixed positive lambda, beta_lambda minimizes the population regularized criterion with the limiting auxiliaries and independently fixed learning objects. Local inference assumes independent matched sets, fixed finite dictionaries, finite relevant moments, interior auxiliary parameters, nonsingular auxiliary information, positive local criterion curvature and a consistent local solution. The covariance is H^-1 Var(S_i) H^-T/N, estimated by centered matched-set influence rows and the N(N-1) denominator. S_i includes the direct criterion score and the full g and f estimation influence across all training folds. Conditional logistic information supplies g influence. Weighted normal equations supply f influence. The same formula applies to saturated finite cells and the finite spline conditional-mean design.

An exposure contrast has design row d=phi(x)-phi(b), log OR=d' beta and variance d' V d. Exponentiating its estimate and Wald limits gives OR and pointwise interval. Single-coordinate curves hold the remaining coordinates at a specified background. Joint curves follow complete supplied combinations or marginal-quantile paths.

Variable participation uses Q_j=n_eval^-1 sum_l (partial phi(x_l)/partial z_j)(partial phi(x_l)/partial z_j)' and statistic beta' Q_j beta. Under the working null Q_j^(1/2) beta_lambda=0, its reference is sum_r eig_r(V^(1/2) Q_j V^(1/2)) chi-square_1,r. Davies integration, retry accuracy and a seeded Monte Carlo fallback evaluate its tail. Holm correction is applied to the specified family; numerical tolerance perturbations assess the decision's stability. In a positive-distance finite Gaussian space, zero coordinate derivative on an open set entails zero centered response. Full-rank finite-grid Q_j gives the zero-coefficient working null. The simulation archive evaluates generating active/null recognition for this screening rule.

Four-point interaction is h(x_j,x_k,b_rest)-h(x_j,b_k,b_rest)-h(b_j,x_k,b_rest)+h(b). Mean squared grid contrasts use the same quadratic-form principle. Pointwise directions follow the signed contrast. Separate Holm families cover overall pairs and eligible grid points.

Baseline Shapley allocation averages each coordinate's incremental contrast over all joining orders. Gaussian separability converts the subset average to a polynomial integral, evaluated exactly by ceiling(p/2)-node Gauss-Legendre quadrature. Contributions sum to the specified log-OR contrast. Mean absolute contributions normalized over the chosen coordinates define overall shares.

Configuration selection learns each candidate kernel on independent learning data, cross-fits auxiliary functions inside each outer training fold, and scores held-out conditional negative log likelihood. Complete matched sets preserve all partitions. A fresh estimation sample uses the selected geometry and configuration. Covariance is conditional on that independent selection.
